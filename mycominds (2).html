About usWelcome to MycoMinds!

Are you fascinated by the hidden world of fungi and microorganisms? Do you want to explore the incredible diversity and importance of these tiny yet mighty organisms?

Our club provides a platform for students to engage in hands-on research, take part in thought-provoking discussions, and collaborate on innovative projects. Whether you’re a seasoned science enthusiast or just beginning your journey, MycoMinds offers a welcoming and stimulating environment for all.

1. Workshops and seminars

2. Research opportunities

Conduct experiments and studies on various fungi and microorganisms.

3. Field trips

Explore natural habitats and collect samples for study.
